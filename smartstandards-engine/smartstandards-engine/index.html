SmartStandards-Engine — internal SIH demo build. Standards data is illustrative and hand-curated for the Electrical & Lighting Equipment category (LED luminaires, cables, LV switchgear). Confidence scores are computed from a transparent weighted formula over retrieval, attribute-match, and graph-relationship sub-scores — not a learned/opaque model. Below 70% confidence, the system flags results for human verification rather than asserting an answer.
